## Notes

`scripts/docs/descriptions.yml` and `docs/reference/ports.md` carry the same port facts in table form.

The commented `jlinc-server`, `jlinc-db`, `fedid-*` services and Traefik labels in `docker-compose.yml` are off, so they are not drawn.

Line 2 of each `.mmd` here sets the top-level `fontFamily`. `fontFamily` under `themeVariables` in `_init.txt` and `vconic-theme.json` does not reach the root of the SVG with mermaid-cli 12.2.0: the SVG then paints in the browser default serif and clips labels measured at a different width. Line 1 stays the exact `_init.txt` line.
